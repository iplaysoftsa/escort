Booking information

Terms & deposits

Please confirm all details directly with Red Room before arranging a booking.

Adult use only

Red Room is for adults aged 18 and over. Profiles and bookings must comply with all applicable South African law.

Availability

Profile details are indicative and can change. The team must confirm a companion's live availability, location and arrival time.

Deposit

A R50 booking deposit is added to every booking unless the team confirms otherwise in writing. A deposit does not guarantee a booking until the team confirms it.

Rates and travel

Confirm the full rate, duration, travel fee, cancellation terms and payment method before arrival.

Respect and safety

Mutual consent, respectful communication and personal safety are required. The team may decline or stop a booking that violates these conditions.
