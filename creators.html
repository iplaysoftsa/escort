The process

How to get started

Creator access is reviewed and invitation-only. Start by requesting the current written terms and onboarding steps.

01

Make an enquiry

Contact Red Room to confirm creator eligibility, booking terms, payout timing and the secure verification process.

02

Complete age and identity checks

Provide an accepted government ID and one live verification photo only through the secure submission route confirmed by Red Room. These checks are separate from public profile photos.

03

Review and sign the agreement

Read the current companion agreement, confirm you are 18+ and participation is voluntary, and review the written safety, conduct, consent, $0.10 profile-view CPM, R1,000 payout threshold and payout terms.

04

Prepare your profile

Share your preferred display name, South African service areas, availability, bio, approved images and media links. Confirm your rates, travel limits and boundaries.

05

Schedule your photoshoot

After the initial checks, expect a call from Red Room to arrange a photoshoot date. Keep your phone reachable and agree on a time that works for you.

06

Review, publish and maintain

Review and approve the photos and final listing before publication. Use your invitation to sign in and keep permitted profile details current.

Before applying

Eligibility and profile requirements

◆ Adults only · KYC

You must be at least 18. Accepted ID may include a South African Smart ID, ID book, passport or driver’s licence. The live-photo check uses a handwritten note with the current date and “Red Room KYC”. No minors may apply, appear in media or be involved in any service.

◆ Consent and image rights

You must approve publication of your own profile and have permission for every image or video supplied. Everyone shown must be an adult and have consented to that use.

◆ Accurate, complete details

Provide a display name, approved contact details, service areas, availability, rates, biography and any optional attributes you want shown. Keep details truthful and up to date.

◆ Signed agreement

Before your profile goes live or earnings are released, sign the companion agreement covering voluntary participation, services, boundaries, safety, conduct, media consent and payout terms.

Outcall bookings

Booking rates, commission and deposit

The concierge confirms the booking and approved payment instructions. Do not travel or treat a booking as confirmed based only on a payment screenshot.

Current guide rates: from R500 for one hour and R1,500 overnight. The R50 booking deposit is paid upfront in addition to the service rate. Red Room retains 20% of the service amount; creators receive 80% of the service amount after payment is confirmed. The deposit and separately quoted driver/travel fees are excluded from this split.

01

Confirm the details

Agree the date, time, duration, area, service rate and boundaries with the team. If a driver is requested, confirm the separately quoted distance-based driver/travel fee before accepting.

02

Deposit and proof

The client pays the R50 booking deposit upfront, in addition to the service price, using the payment method and reference provided, then sends proof of payment. The concierge verifies receipt before confirming or dispatching.

03

Service payment and creator share

The client settles the full service amount and any confirmed driver/travel fee as instructed; the R50 deposit is additional. After the service amount is marked paid, the creator receives 80% of that service amount. The deposit and driver/travel fee are excluded from the commission calculation.

04

Changes and no-shows

Under the written policy, the deposit may help offset wasted time and travel after a late cancellation or no-show. Rescheduling, refunds and unfulfilled bookings follow the agreed rules.

Protect your privacy

Keep personal information private

  • Do not place your home address, ID number, banking details or private meeting details on a public profile.
  • Never share a client's identity, contact details or private messages publicly.
  • Verify the agency contact independently before sending identity or payment information.
  • Never share your password or sign-in link; creator access is personal and invitation-only.
  • Send ID and live-photo KYC only through the secure route confirmed by management; ask about access, storage and deletion.
  • Agree your boundaries and booking details before confirming an arrangement.
  • Use only images you have approved and can lawfully share.

Transparent by design

What to confirm in writing

Booking commission is 20% to Red Room and 80% to the creator on the paid service amount. Keep the written agreement and confirm payment timing and cancellation rules with management.

  • The R1,000 payout threshold, selected EFT/e-wallet method and expected transfer timing.
  • Who receives and handles the R50 deposit, and the cancellation, refund and rescheduling rules.
  • Driver/travel fees are quoted separately based on distance and excluded from the 20% booking commission calculation.
  • How client enquiries, bookings and availability are confirmed.
  • What profile content is published and how to request a correction or removal.
  • How to contact the team and raise a safety or payment concern.
!

Safety, consent and confidentiality

There is zero tolerance for abuse, coercion, non-consensual content or underage involvement. Keep ID documents and agreements confidential and separate from public media. Contact management immediately if a booking feels unsafe or outside your agreed boundaries. A profile, view counter or content listing does not guarantee bookings or income.
